This guide is for KTN’s volunteer teachers. Your portal has five tabs — Today, Attendance, Marks, Work, Class link — covering everything you do each week.

📲 Install KTN as an app on your phone

It’s free — no app store, no download, no account needed to install. Just add it from your browser and it works like a normal app (own icon, full screen).

🤖 Android (use Chrome)
⋮
ktn-digital-online-school…
  1. Open ktn-digital-online-school.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install / Add to confirm.
✓ The KTN icon appears on your home screen.
🍎 iPhone & iPad (use Safari)
⤴
ktn-digital-online-school…
  1. Open the link in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (□ with an ↑ arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add (top-right).
✓ The KTN icon appears on your home screen.
Important: on iPhone you must use Safari to install; on Android use Chrome. The app needs an internet connection to work. To open it later, just tap the KTN icon — no need to type the address again.

The admin creates your account with a temporary password.

  1. Tap Sign in, enter your email and the temporary password.
  2. Prefer to set your own? Tap Forgot password, get the email link, and choose a new password.
  3. You land on your Today dashboard, showing a badge like “Teaching since 2019 · N years”.

  1. Open the Attendance tab (or tap Take attendance on Today).
  2. If you teach more than one class, pick the class at the top.
  3. Choose the date (defaults to today).
  4. Tap each student to set Present / Absent / Late.
  5. Tap Save. Parents and students see it immediately in their attendance ring.

  1. Open the Marks tab.
  2. Pick the class and the semester (Sem 1 / Sem 2).
  3. Type each student’s score (out of 100).
  4. Tap Save. Students see it under their Marks, and it feeds the report card.
Note: you can only enter marks for your own subject and grade(s) — this keeps records safe.

To post an assignment:

  1. Tap New assignment.
  2. Choose the grade, add a title, optional instructions, and a due date.
  3. Upload worksheet (goes straight to the KTN Google Drive) — or paste a link if uploads aren’t set up.
  4. Tap Post assignment.

To review submissions:

  1. Tap View class on an assignment.
  2. See each student’s status — Pending / Submitted / Done.
  3. Open a student’s uploaded work, then tap Mark done to confirm.

The Class link tab is where you set the online meeting link students use.

  1. Paste your Zoom / Meet link and tap Save — it appears on students’ pages automatically.
  2. Use Copy link to share it elsewhere.

If you can’t hold a class:

  1. Tick Class cancelled and type a reason (e.g. “Teacher unwell today”).
  2. Tap Save. Students see “No class — [reason]” instead of the link.
  3. It clears automatically the next day — no need to untick it.
🦉 Tip: cancel on the day of the class you’re missing.
